Experiments

Interactive demos. Open each one in the browser and watch the metric.

  • LCP Image

    ~25 variants of an LCP image: lazy, preload, fetchpriority, AVIF/WebP, picture tag, video poster, and more. Average LCP per variant is recorded in session storage.

    View live demo Source

  • View Transitions

    Measure how the auto view-transition affects LCP across navigations. Collects 100 LCP samples and reports the average for transitioned vs non-transitioned variants.

    View live demo Source

  • dataLayer scheduled after paint

    Drop-in script that defers dataLayer.push until after the next paint, so synchronous GTM work doesn't blow up INP during interactions.

    View live demo Source
